### Applied Research
<ul class="research-list">
  <li>
    <a href="/research/appetite" target="_blank">Polyunsaturated Fatty Acids Promote Appetite via the Microbiome-Gut-Brain Axis</a>
    <small>(with <a href="https://liowyijia.github.io/" target="_blank">Yi Jia Liow</a>, <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=Dcv_TJ8AAAAJ" target="_blank">Mustafa Talay</a>, Vladimir Yeliseyev, <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=EQM8r_UAAAAJ" target="_blank">Lynn Bry</a>, and <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=2Fnk6fQAAAAJ" target="_blank">Rachel N. Carmody</a>)</small>
    <div class="publication-note"><em>bioRxiv</em> preprint, 2025.</div>
    <div class="research-note">A diet-microbiome-gut-brain model showing that specific industrialized diet components, especially PUFAs, can increase appetite by altering gut microbiota and brain serotonin metabolism.</div>
  </li>
  <li>
    <a href="/research/conjoint-constitution" target="_blank">Constitutional Design Preferences: An Experimental Approach</a>
    <small>(with <a href="https://www.kennethmcelwain.com/" target="_blank">Kenneth Mori McElwain</a>, Ai Takahashi, and <a href="https://researchmap.jp/cgw/?lang=en" target="_blank">Christian G. Winkler</a>)</small>
    <div class="publication-note"><em>International Journal of Constitutional Law</em>, 2024.</div>
    <div class="research-note">A conjoint analysis showing that Japanese citizens prefer detailed constitutions, especially human rights provisions, and view amendment flexibility as a strategic safeguard.</div>
  </li>
  <li>
    <a href="/research/conjoint-age" target="_blank">Just a Number? Voter Evaluations of Age in Candidate Choice Experiments</a>
    <small>(with <a href="https://sites.google.com/site/danielmarkhamsmith/home" target="_blank">Daniel M. Smith</a>)</small>
    <div class="publication-note"><em>The Journal of Politics</em>, 2022</div>
    <div class="research-note">A meta-analysis and conjoint experiment showing that voters across democracies consistently penalize older political candidates, even when age differences are substantively small.</div>
  </li>
  <li>
    <a href="/research/constitution-proposer-proposal/" target="_blank">The Proposer or the Proposal? An Experimental Analysis of Constitutional Beliefs</a>
    <small>(with <a href="https://www.kennethmcelwain.com/" target="_blank">Kenneth Mori McElwain</a> and <a href="https://researchmap.jp/cgw/?lang=en" target="_blank">Christian G. Winkler</a>)</small>
    <div class="publication-note"><em>Japanese Journal of Political Science</em>, 2021</div>
    <div class="research-note">A survey experiment on constitutional amendment preferences showing that Japanese citizens favor non-partisan proposals, while constitutional ideology shapes support across issue areas independently of partisanship.</div>
  </li>
</ul>
